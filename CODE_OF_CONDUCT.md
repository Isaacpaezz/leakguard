# Code of conduct

leakguard adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers (many of them write in Spanish),
criticize ideas rather than people, and never share another person's private
data, including IP addresses or VPN details.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@Isaacpaezz](https://github.com/Isaacpaezz), through a
[private advisory](https://github.com/Isaacpaezz/leakguard/security/advisories/new).
The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block participants who
break these rules.
